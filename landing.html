Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to report an item?

Yes. A free account helps us verify reports and notify you about possible matches.

How do I claim an item I found listed?

Open the item, click "Claim", and answer the verification questions. Once approved, you'll get instructions for pickup.

Where are found items kept?

Found items are turned over to the designated Lost & Found office. Pickup details appear after your claim is approved.

How long are unclaimed items kept?

Unclaimed items are held for a set period (check with your office) before being donated or disposed of.
